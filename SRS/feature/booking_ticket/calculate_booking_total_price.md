# Calculate Booking Total Price

### User Story

As the System, I can calculate the total price of a booking based on the selected tickets and their prices so that the customer knows how much they need to pay.

### Acceptance Criteria

• The system calculates the total price of a booking by summing up the prices of all selected tickets.
• The system applies any applicable discounts or promotions to the total price.
• The system displays the calculated total price to the customer during the booking process.
